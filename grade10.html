For the teacher

Suggested pacing: two 45–60 minute lessons. Begin each section by asking the student to predict an answer. Let her explore, then ask her to explain why.

Bring a ruler and a small object for a hands-on measurement. Screen diagrams teach scale reading; they are not physical measuring tools.

Use a number and a unit. Always ask: how reliable is this measurement?
